Motorhome stopovers

Park your motorhome on the island in the heart of the Karlskrona archipelago, three kilometres from town. Choose a pitch among the greenery or right by the water.

How to find the right pitch

The motorhome pitches are marked with a motorhome symbol and pitch number on the site map. Each pitch has its own fire safety plan for how the motorhome must be parked. You can see it on the map and on the barrier card you get at check-in, and we will also tell you when you check in.

The pitches right by the water are in the Red zone and cannot be booked in advance. They are allocated on arrival, subject to availability. If you would prefer to stand there, book a pitch in another zone to be safe, especially in summer.

Services at the campsite

  • Service building with shower and toilet
  • Laundry room
  • Kitchen
  • Wifi
  • Water emptying and filling
  • Camperclean for toilet cassettes

Washing machine, tumble dryer and Camperclean (SEK 20) are paid at check-out. Camperclean empties, rinses and refills the cassette with the right amount of water and a microbiological agent.
