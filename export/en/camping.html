Camping with the sea around the corner

The campsite stretches like a U around the island, divided into small fields. The largest has only 52 pitches. The west side is lush and green, the east side looks out over Karlskrona.

All caravan and motorhome pitches have electricity included in the price. Around the island there are three swimming spots: two sandy beaches and a rocky shore with a jetty.
