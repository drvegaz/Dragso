Getting here

Dragsö is three kilometres from Karlskrona's city centre, with a bridge from the mainland. You can get here by car, on foot, by bike or on the archipelago boat.

How to get here

Address
Dragsövägen 14
371 37 Karlskrona
Open in Google Maps ↗
Car
Over the bridge from Karlskrona. Park in the check-in lane on the right when you arrive, and don't drive up to the barrier – it is the only way in.
Walking & cycling
A paved footpath and cycle path all the way into town, about 20 minutes on foot.
Boat
In summer the archipelago boats run between Dragsö and the city centre on behalf of Blekingetrafiken.

Nearby

Day trips from Dragsö

Karlskrona

The World Heritage town with Stortorget, the Naval Museum and Kungsholm Fortress, 20 minutes on foot.

ARK56

Trails in the Blekinge Archipelago for cycling, hiking, kayaking and boating, with an app that shows the way.

Hittaut

A map or app with checkpoints to find on your walk, bike ride or run.

Wämöparken

A park with a mini zoo and walking paths just outside Karlskrona.
