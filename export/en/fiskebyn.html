Fiskebyn

Five south-facing cabins with a large patio and a view of Karlskrona. 35 metres to the archipelago boat and 90 metres to the beach.

How you stay

The bedroom has two bunk beds, and the living room has a sofa bed for two. The living room and kitchen are one room, and the bathroom has a shower and toilet.

The patio is large and looks out over the town. You park right next to the cabin.

Equipment

  • Shower and toilet
  • Fridge and freezer
  • Dishwasher
  • Cooker, oven and microwave
  • Crockery, cutlery and pans
  • Coffee maker and kettle
  • TV
  • Duvets and pillows
  • Iron
  • Outdoor furniture
  • Wifi

Good to know when you book

In high and peak season the cabin is booked Saturday to Saturday. Single nights only by exception.
