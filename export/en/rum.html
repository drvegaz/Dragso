Hostel rooms

Four rooms with their own entrance, just ten metres from the beach. Final cleaning is included in the price.

How you stay

The rooms are 8 m² and have a bunk bed with a pull-out lower bed, for two or three. Shower, toilet and a kitchen with cooker, oven, fridge, freezer and dishwasher are in the service building, 35 metres away, which also has a dining room. Bring your own kitchen equipment if you want to cook there.

Equipment

  • Fridge with a small freezer compartment
  • TV
  • Coffee maker
  • Mugs, glasses, plates and cutlery
  • Duvets and pillows
