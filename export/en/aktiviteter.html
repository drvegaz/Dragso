Activities

Paddle out into the archipelago, warm up in the sauna on the raft or play mini golf through the World Heritage site. You rent and book most things at reception.

Summer

Summer programme and Kids Club

In summer we fill the programme with activities for the whole family: music quizzes, karaoke, sing-alongs, ghost hunts, after beach for kids and much more. This week's programme is available at reception.

Kids Club two days a week in high summer, with one session for children under 7 and one for children aged 7 and over. Suits children aged 4–12, but everyone who wants to is welcome to join.

The sauna raft

Wood-fired sauna and hot tub on the water

Rent the sauna raft with family, friends or colleagues. It is moored at the jetty on Dragsö, with parking nearby, and can be booked all year round for up to 11 people.

Mini golf

Adventure mini golf and billiard golf

The adventure mini golf is Karlskrona's World Heritage site in miniature: 18 holes past Kungsholm Fortress, the Ropewalk and Grevagården, with Rosenbom and Karl XI. Billiard golf is a standing-height golf course played with a billiard cue, also 18 holes.
