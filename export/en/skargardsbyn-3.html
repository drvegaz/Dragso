Skärgårdsbyn, three beds

Four cabins built like old-fashioned boathouses, 25 metres from the beach. One room of 14 m² with a kitchenette, a double bed and an upper bunk.

How you stay

The room has a double bed with a built-in bunk above it, and a dining table for four. Shower and toilet are in the service building, 50 metres away.

Outside there is a terrace with a bench, tables and benches to eat at, and two shared brick barbecues.

Equipment

  • Fridge with a small freezer compartment
  • Crockery, cutlery and pans
  • Coffee maker
  • Duvets and pillows
  • Washing-up and cleaning equipment
  • TV
  • Outdoor furniture
  • Shared brick barbecues

Good to know when you book

In high and peak season the cabin is booked Saturday to Saturday. Single nights only by exception.
