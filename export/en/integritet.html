Privacy and cookies

This website does not collect information about you when you visit it. Here we describe what applies when you book or get in touch.

Cookies and statistics

The website uses no cookies for visitors. We don't measure traffic, show no advertising and load nothing from other companies, not even fonts. That is why we don't ask about cookies when you arrive.

Only people who log in to edit the website get a cookie, which is needed for the login.

When you book

Bookings are made in our booking system at boka.dragso.se. There you provide the information needed for the booking, and it is used to manage your stay.

When you get in touch

If you email or call us, we use the information to reply and help you with your stay.

Links to other websites

We link to Google Maps, the booking system and Restaurang Havsviken, among others. When you follow such a link, that website's own rules apply.

Your rights

You have the right to know what information we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Get in touch and we will help you. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
