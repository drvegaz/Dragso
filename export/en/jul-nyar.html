Christmas and New Year on Dragsö

We are open over Christmas and New Year. Celebrate in a warm cabin on the island, within walking distance of Karlskrona and with activities for both children and adults.

Activities over Christmas and New Year

All activities cost SEK 20 per person and are paid with Swish on site.

For children

Ubbe & Smilla's treasure mystery

Find the lost clues on the island and solve the mystery. For children aged 3–8, and everyone who solves it gets a share of the treasure.

Christmas bingo

Find the pictures on the bingo card. For children aged 3–5, with a secret prize drawn afterwards.

For adults

Quiz walk

A quiz walk around the island throughout the period.

Reflector trail

Follow the reflectors and make the secret word. Come when it gets dark and bring a torch.

Stay in a cabin over the holidays

Celebrating Christmas in Karlskrona or getting away for New Year? The cabins with shower, toilet and a fully equipped kitchen are a good fit, and Örlogsbyn has underfloor heating in every room.

Book online and save up to SEK 90.

The sauna raft

Warm up in the wood-fired sauna and hot tub out on the water, with family or friends. The sauna raft can be booked all year round.
