Världsarvsbyn

Ten comfortable cabins in the middle of the campsite, 30–80 metres from the beach. 26 m² with shower, toilet, kitchen and a private brick barbecue.

How you stay

The bedroom has two beds and two fold-down beds, and the living room has a sofa bed for two.

The covered veranda has outdoor furniture and a private brick barbecue. You park right next to the cabin. Two of the cabins are wheelchair accessible, with a ramp and an adapted shower room.

Equipment

  • Shower and toilet
  • Fridge and freezer
  • Dishwasher
  • Cooker, oven and microwave
  • Crockery, cutlery and pans
  • Coffee maker and kettle
  • TV and DVD
  • Duvets and pillows
  • Iron
  • Underfloor heating
  • Private brick barbecue
  • Wifi

Good to know when you book

In high and peak season the cabin is booked Saturday to Saturday. Single nights only by exception.
