Cabin suites

Five cabins with their own themes, shower, toilet and a fully equipped kitchen. 36 m² with a bedroom, sleeping loft and underfloor heating in every room.

Five cabins, five themes

Each Cabin suite has its own interior.

  • 27ArchipelagoAn exclusive kitchen and lots of wood, like a modern archipelago cottage.
  • 28RetroModern retro with an exclusive kitchen.Pets allowed
  • 29MountainLike an alpine chalet, with stone and dark wood.
  • 30Black/whiteEverything in black and white, with a piano-lacquered kitchen.
  • 31GlassGlass in pictures, sculptures and details.

How you stay

Master bedroom with a double bed, loft with two single beds and a sofa bed in the living room. Flat-screen TV in the living room and bedroom.

The covered veranda has outdoor furniture, and you park right next to the cabin.
