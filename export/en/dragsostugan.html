Dragsöstugan

The traditional Dragsöstugan is in the middle of the campsite, 60 metres from the beach. One room of 16 m² with its own deck and barbecue.

How you stay

The sleeping area has a bunk bed and a sofa bed for two. The kitchen area has hotplates, a coffee maker, a fridge and a table for four. Shower, toilet and dishwasher are in the service building, 10 metres away.

Equipment

  • Fridge with freezer compartment
  • Hotplates and microwave
  • Crockery, cutlery and pans
  • Coffee maker
  • TV
  • Outdoor furniture and barbecue
  • Duvets and pillows
  • Washing-up and cleaning equipment

Good to know when you book

In high and peak season the cabin is booked Saturday to Saturday. Single nights only by exception.
