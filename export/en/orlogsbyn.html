Örlogsbyn

Eight naval-themed cabins with shower, toilet and a fully equipped kitchen. 44 m² with a bedroom, sleeping loft and underfloor heating in every room.

Eight cabins, eight themes

Each cabin is furnished after a role or a ship from the naval town of Karlskrona.

How you stay

Master bedroom with a double bed, loft with two single beds and a sofa bed in the living room. There is a smart TV in the living room, bedroom and loft.

The veranda has outdoor furniture, and you can park up to two cars right next to the cabin. EV charging is available.
